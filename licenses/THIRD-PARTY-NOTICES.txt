Part 2. FunkyPatcher.exe (a separate program)
---------------------------------------------

FunkyPatcher builds the Russian item-name patches on the player's PC (Starfield, Fallout 4). It is downloaded
from the launcher's GitHub release of the same version and runs as a separate process.
License: GPL-3.0 (GPL-3.0.txt). Source: https://github.com/FunkyPlay/FunkyLauncher/tree/main/src/FunkyPatcher
Its components: FunkyPatcher-NOTICES.txt

Part 4. Games and mods
----------------------

The games belong to their publishers. The launcher does not ship game files.
Every mod belongs to its author. Mods are downloaded by the player from their pages on Nexus Mods (or the
author's own page) under the author's terms; every mod of a modlist with a link to its page (author, license,
permissions) is in the launcher's window "Mods of <modlist>". Our own small patches are made by FunkyPlay or, for item names, built on the player's PC.
